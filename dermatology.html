Hair Loss Solutions

PRP therapy

Platelet-Rich Plasma

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy is one of our most effective non-surgical treatments for hair thinning and early-stage hair loss. A small sample of the patient's own blood is drawn and processed to concentrate its platelets, which are then injected into the scalp. The growth factors released stimulate dormant follicles, extend the hair's natural growth phase and improve density over a course of sessions. Because the treatment uses the patient's own blood, it carries minimal risk and requires no downtime — making it a popular, medically-grounded option for both men and women noticing early signs of thinning.

Laser Hair Removal

Smooth, without the routine

Put an end to slow, messy and painful hair removal methods such as shaving, waxing, tweezing and creams. Our Xlase Machine uses short, safe bursts of light from a cold tip to remove unwanted hair from all body sites.

Does it hurt?

Pain tolerance is very individual. No anaesthetic is required — most people describe the sensation as similar to a cold ice cube moving across the skin.

Precautions before & after?

Shave 24–48 hours before treatment, and avoid plucking, waxing or hair-removal cream for four weeks after. Avoid sun and self-tanning before and during treatment.

How many sessions?

Clinical studies confirm at least 3–6 treatments are needed, spaced 1–3 months apart. Call +964-750-900-0200 to discuss your plan.
