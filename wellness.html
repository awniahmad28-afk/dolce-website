IV Therapy & Booster Shots

What is IV therapy?

IV therapy delivers nutrients and hydration directly into the bloodstream for immediate absorption — a 90–100% absorption rate versus only 20–50% orally.

  1. 01

    Hydration Drip

    Improves skin, joints and muscles. Keeps your immune system strong and fights infection.

  2. 02

    Detox Drip

    Aids the body's ability to absorb minerals, protects against toxic chemicals, and offers anti-ageing benefits.

  3. 03

    Anti-Aging Drip

    Designed to slow visible signs of aging: packed with antioxidants to fight free radicals, supports skin elasticity and firmness, and promotes cellular repair and rejuvenation.

  4. 04

    Power Drip

    An instant energy and wellness recharge: boosts physical and mental energy levels, enhances immune system performance, and restores hydration and essential vitamins for overall vitality.

  5. 05

    Radiant Skin Drip

    A powerful blend of vitamins and antioxidants that brightens dull skin and promotes a healthy glow, supports collagen production for smoother, firmer skin, and helps reduce signs of fatigue and stress on the skin.

  6. 06

    Hair Boost Drip

    Specially formulated to nourish from within: strengthens hair follicles and reduces shedding, promotes healthier, shinier and thicker hair, and restores essential nutrients that support hair growth.

  7. 07

    NAD+ Drip

    NAD+ IV therapy supports cellular energy, metabolism, and healthy cell function. It may also help support mental clarity, focus, and the body’s natural repair processes.

  8. 08

    Royal Flush Drip

    The Royal Flush IV Drip combines the benefits of hydration and detox support in one treatment. Designed to replenish fluids and essential nutrients while supporting your body’s natural detoxification processes, leaving you feeling refreshed and revitalized.

  9. 09

    Booster Shots

    Energy (B12), Vitamin D and Biotin shots — for immediate effect, immune support and healthier skin, nails and hair.
