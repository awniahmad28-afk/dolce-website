Laser Hair Removal

Put an end to slow, messy and painful hair removal methods such as shaving, waxing, tweezing and creams. Our Xlase Machine uses short, safe bursts of light from a cold tip to remove unwanted hair from all body sites.

Frequently asked

Does it hurt?

Pain tolerance is very individual. No anaesthetic is required — most people describe the sensation as similar to a cold ice cube moving across the skin.

Do I need precautions before & after treatment?

Shave 24–48 hours before treatment so the laser can catch the hair root, and avoid plucking, tweezing, waxing or hair-removal cream for four weeks after. Avoid sun, solarium or self-tanning before and during treatment.

How many sessions do I need?

Growth cycles vary by body site, hair density, ethnic origin, hormones and age. Clinical studies confirm at least 3–6 treatments are needed, spaced 1–3 months apart. Call +964-750-900-0200 to discuss your plan.

Plastic Surgery

Face

If sagging, loose facial skin or stubborn wrinkles — or a nose that isn't quite the shape you'd like — are on your mind, our facial procedures are designed to rejuvenate your appearance and restore your confidence. Whether your concerns sit in the upper, middle or lower face and neck, our experienced doctors help find the right procedure, prioritising the safest, highest-quality results.

  • Upper & lower blepharoplasty — eyelid surgery to remove excess skin, tighten and create an eyelid crease
  • Face lift (rhytidectomy) — addresses sagging and wrinkles in the mid-to-lower face by tightening underlying tissue and removing excess skin
  • Neck lift (lower rhytidectomy) — often performed with a face lift for a harmonious result
  • Brow lift (forehead lift) — elevates a drooping eyebrow
  • Rhinoplasty — adjusts the size, shape and projection of the nose
  • Lip lift — removes a section of tissue along the base of the nose for a fuller, more youthful pout

Can I undergo more than one facial procedure?

Yes — if desired, we can combine two or more procedures into one surgery for a more comprehensive, balanced result, with less overall downtime and lower cost than separate surgeries.

Body

Aesthetic body surgeries significantly alter and improve the appearance of the body — for areas affected by weight change, pregnancy or excess skin.   • Breast augmentation — improves breast size or shape

IV Therapy & Booster Shots

What is IV therapy?
IV therapy delivers nutrients and hydration directly into the bloodstream for immediate absorption — a 90–100% absorption rate versus only 20–50% orally. Lead a healthy, balanced, enriched lifestyle with our specially formulated injections and IV therapies, packed with minerals, antioxidants, vitamins and nutrients to leave you re-energised.

Hydration Drip

Improves skin, joints and muscles. Keeps your immune system strong and fights infection. Helps your body recover and rehydrate.

Detox Drip

Aids the body's ability to absorb minerals, protects against toxic chemicals, and offers anti-ageing benefits — good for liver, kidney, high cholesterol, heart disease and hepatitis. Helps ease nausea, pain and inflammation.

Booster Shots

Energy Booster (Vitamin B12) — delivered intramuscularly for immediate effect. Vitamin D — supports immune function, stronger bones and teeth, and improved mood. Biotin — the "keratin" shot for dry skin, brittle nails and hair, and hair loss.
