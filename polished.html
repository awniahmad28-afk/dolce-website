About Polished

Beauty, to the Dolce standard

Polished by Dolce is the beauty salon of Dolce Aesthetic Clinic, set within the same Park View address in Erbil. It brings the clinic’s care, precision and attention to detail to every hair, nail and beauty appointment.

From colour and blow-dry styling to manicures, lashes and brows, each service is delivered by a skilled team in a calm, inviting space made for you to unwind and take your time.

Our aim is simple: that you leave looking refined, feeling cared for, and entirely yourself.
