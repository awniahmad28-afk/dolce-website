Plastic Surgery

If sagging, loose facial skin or stubborn wrinkles — or a nose that isn't quite the shape you'd like — are on your mind, our facial and body procedures are designed to rejuvenate your appearance and restore your confidence, prioritising the safest, highest-quality results.

Face

  • Upper & Lower BlepharoplastyEyelid surgery to remove excess skin, tighten and create an eyelid crease.
  • Face Lift (Rhytidectomy)Addresses sagging and wrinkles in the mid-to-lower face by tightening underlying tissue and removing excess skin.
  • Neck LiftOften performed with a face lift for a harmonious result.
  • Brow LiftElevates a drooping eyebrow.
  • RhinoplastyAdjusts the size, shape and projection of the nose.
  • Lip LiftRemoves a section of tissue along the base of the nose for a fuller, more youthful pout.

Body

Aesthetic body surgeries significantly alter and improve the appearance of the body — for areas affected by weight change, pregnancy or excess skin. Every procedure is individualised with our specialist surgeon during your consultation.

  • LiposuctionRemoves fat from areas such as the abdomen, arms, thighs and chin.
  • Breast AugmentationImproves breast size or shape.
  • Fat GraftingRemoves fat from one area and uses it to enhance another.
  • Tummy TuckFlattens, smooths and tightens the abdomen.

Can I undergo more than one facial procedure?

If desired, we can combine two or more procedures into one surgery to achieve more comprehensive facial rejuvenation and a balanced look. Combining procedures also has the benefit of less overall downtime and lower costs compared to undergoing multiple separate surgeries.
